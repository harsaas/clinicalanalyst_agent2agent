Recipe 1: Quick Black Beans and Brown Rice
Ingredients:
- 1 cup instant brown rice
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- Salt to taste
- Pepper to taste
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
Steps (10):
1) Cook the instant brown rice according to package instructions.
2) Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
3) Add chopped onion and sauté until translucent.
4) Stir in minced garlic and cook for 1 minute.
5) Add black beans to the pan and stir well.
6) Season with cumin, chili powder, salt, and pepper.
7) Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
8) Remove from heat and stir in cooked rice.
9) Garnish with chopped cilantro.
10) Serve warm.

Recipe 2: Spicy Pinto Beans and Quinoa
Ingredients:
- 1 cup quinoa
- 1 can pinto beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 red bell pepper, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- Salt to taste
- Pepper to taste
- 1/4 cup chopped green onions
Steps (10):
1) Cook quinoa according to package instructions.
2) Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
3) Add chopped red bell pepper and sauté until soft.
4) Stir in minced garlic and cook for 1 minute.
5) Add pinto beans to the pan and mix well.
6) Season with smoked paprika, cayenne pepper, salt, and pepper.
7) Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
8) Remove from heat and stir in cooked quinoa.
9) Garnish with chopped green onions.
10) Serve immediately.
